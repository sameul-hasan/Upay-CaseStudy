Merchant Acquisition

E-Commerce Checkout & Platform Integration Guide

E-commerce merchants adopt payment channels based on three metrics: conversion rate, integration speed, and settlement turnaround. Here is how upay can establish merchant leadership.

1. In-App Embedded Checkout (iFrame / Popup)

Keeps the shopper on the merchant's domain. Reduces cart abandonment by opening an encrypted modal overlay for mobile number input and PIN verification.

Conversion Lift: +18% vs Redirects
Ideal For: Apparel, Electronics, Food Delivery

2. Dynamic Bangla QR for Desktop Web

When checking out on a PC or laptop, generates an interoperable Bangla QR code. The user scans it using their upay or any inter-bank MFS app to authorize payment in 5 seconds.

Interoperability: Universal Bangla QR
Eliminates: Manual OTP typing on desktop

3. Tokenized Recurring Billing (Auto-Pay)

One-time OTP authorization generates a cryptographically signed billing token. Enables 1-click repeat orders and automatic subscriptions for SaaS, tuition, and internet bills.

Use Cases: Broadband, OTT Subscriptions, EdTech
Churn Reduction: -35% Failed Renewals
